Check your Indian Railway PNR status

A PNR is your unique 10-digit booking reference. Use it to check passengers, coach, berth and reservation status.

What CNF, RAC and WL mean

CNF means your seat is confirmed. RAC allows you to board while a berth may be shared. WL means you are in a queue waiting for a cancellation.

What you need

Your 10-digit PNR number.
