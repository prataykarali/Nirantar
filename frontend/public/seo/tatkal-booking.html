Tatkal booking guide

Tatkal is the last-minute railway booking quota. It has limited seats and opens one day before departure from the train origin.

What you need

Passenger details, journey date, train, class and a ready payment method.

Important

AC Tatkal generally opens at 10:00 AM and non-AC Tatkal at 11:00 AM. Check the official service for current availability and rules.
