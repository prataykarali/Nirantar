When does Tatkal booking open?

For a journey departing tomorrow, AC Tatkal typically opens at 10:00 AM and non-AC Tatkal typically opens at 11:00 AM, one day before departure from the train’s origin.

Remember

Seats are limited and rules can change. Verify the live quota and fare on the official IRCTC service before paying.
